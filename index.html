BEFORE THE FIRST MISSION

Questions worth
answering early.

Is AIR MARINE already approved for class surveys?

No class approval is claimed in this beta. The certification pathway and requirements for each service are being evaluated. A mission supporting class would need the relevant approved supplier, accepted method and survey arrangements.

What is the role of a technical partner?

Drone operators capture visual information. The appointed inspector, surveyor or specialist provides the technical assessment and sign-off required by the scope. Responsibilities must be agreed before the work starts.

Can the same equipment cover every application?

Equipment is selected for each mission. Exterior capture, enclosed spaces, thickness measurement and live visual support have different requirements. Specialist applications would be developed with suitable partners.

Where are pilot projects being considered?

The initial market focus is Spain and the Mediterranean, with West Africa as a development region. Availability, local permissions and mobilisation would be assessed for each proposed pilot.

Are the images and examples from completed missions?

No. The images and demonstration footage illustrate the service concept. The evidence preview shows a proposed deliverable format, rather than an actual client inspection or technical finding.
