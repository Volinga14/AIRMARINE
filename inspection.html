01 / VESSELS AND MARINE ASSETS

A clear visual record of your asset.

Visual condition capture for accessible vessel structures, marine infrastructure and areas of interest. Organised to support maintenance planning and technical review.

Plan this project↗

FOR Shipowners, technical managers, port operators and asset teams.

WHERE IT FITS

Typical applications

  • Above-waterline hull and superstructure
  • Deck equipment and accessible external structures
  • Jetties, quays and marine infrastructure
  • Pre-maintenance and post-work visual records

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Agreed outputs

  • Overview photographs linked to areas of interest
  • Indexed close views and location references
  • Coverage register with access limitations
  • A concise mission summary for the appointed reviewer
