02 / MARINE OPERATIONS AND PROJECTS

Keep critical stages in view.

Aerial documentation of selected marine operations, linking key stages and interfaces to a structured visual record for project teams and Marine Warranty Surveyors.

Plan this project↗

FOR Project owners, marine contractors, logistics teams and attending surveyors.

WHERE IT FITS

Typical applications

  • Load-outs and project cargo handling
  • Heavy-lift operations and marine interfaces
  • Berthing, mooring and selected transfer operations
  • Offshore project milestones and marine logistics

WHAT YOU RECEIVE

Agreed outputs

  • Video segments linked to agreed milestones
  • Indexed photographs of relevant interfaces
  • An operation timeline and coverage summary
  • Live visual support where technically feasible

Useful information to start

Operation sequence, critical milestones, site layout, schedule, responsible marine team and flight restrictions.

Scope and responsibilities

The appointed surveyor retains technical judgement and sign-off. Flight activities must fit the operation plan, site controls and permissions; an aerial record does not replace operational supervision.
