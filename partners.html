Practical questions

Is AIR MARINE class approved?

No class approval is claimed in this beta. The approval pathway and requirements for each service are being evaluated. Class-related work requires appropriate supplier approvals and an accepted survey arrangement.

Can specialist inspections be included?

Confined-space work, NDT and thickness measurements are specialist applications to develop with suitable partners. Equipment, personnel and approvals must fit the assignment.

Where are projects being considered?

The initial market focus is Spain and the Mediterranean, with West Africa as a development region. Availability and local permissions are assessed for each mission.

Are the visuals from completed client projects?

No. The generated imagery and demonstration footage illustrate the proposed services. They do not represent AIR MARINE client work or verified technical findings.
