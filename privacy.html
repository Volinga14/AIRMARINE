Information
and privacy.

How this beta website handles the information you enter and the illustrative material it presents.

Local project brief

The enquiry tool processes the fields in your browser to create a text brief. The website does not send the form to AIR MARINE or store it in a database. You choose whether to download or copy the result. Avoid including confidential or sensitive project information in a public demonstration.

Website resources

The site uses local styles, scripts, images and video assets. It does not include analytics scripts, advertising trackers or third-party form services. GitHub Pages provides hosting and may process technical request information as part of delivering the website.

Illustrative material

The AI-generated imagery, demonstration footage and sample deliverable structures illustrate the proposed services. They are not evidence of completed client assignments, approved inspection results or an existing certification.

Service development

This is a beta presentation of AIR MARINE, a KEELCORE initiative. Each assignment requires scope agreement, feasibility review, suitable personnel and equipment, permissions and any relevant specialist or class acceptance arrangements.
